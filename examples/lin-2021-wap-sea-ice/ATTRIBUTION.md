## What was changed

- The article's text was condensed to 59 panels. Each factual caption is the paper's own sentence, sometimes shortened,
  and each cites a claim in `claims.yaml` that quotes the paper.
- Chart values are taken from the paper's text (Results, paragraphs 2 and 6). No figure was read.
- Characters (Wren, Teo, Dot, Kip), their dialogue and the pictures are invented for the comic; none is in the paper.
  The humour is the comic's own, not the authors'.
