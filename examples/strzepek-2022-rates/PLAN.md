# Plan: The Currency Exchange

Source: Strzepek et al. 2022, J. Plankton Res. (CC BY 4.0). A perspective article: no new experiment, an argument.

**Honest story.** There is no discovery and no villain. The tension that is really in the paper is a mismatch of
currencies: models are paid in rates and fluxes (C1, C2); omics arrives with something else (C8, C9, C31); the
case studies show the exchange does not work yet (C19, C27, C52); the authors argue for building a better
exchange rate on purpose (C12, C33, C43). The comic says so in its first caption: it is an argument, not a result.

**Conceit.** The modeller runs a currency exchange desk (the paper's own words: "currencies", "currency converter",
C1, C9, C39). Every gag stays on that metaphor, never on a claim.

**Audience.** Early-career ocean scientists and curious readers. Cast: modeller (guide), physio, omicist, Dot the diatom.
