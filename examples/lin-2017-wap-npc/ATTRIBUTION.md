# Attribution

`storyboard.yaml` in this folder is an adaptation of:

> Lin Y, Cassar N, Marchetti A, Moreno C, Ducklow H, Li Z. **Specific eukaryotic plankton are good
> predictors of net community production in the Western Antarctic Peninsula.**
> *Scientific Reports* 7, 14845 (2017).
> DOI [10.1038/s41598-017-14109-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-14109-1) ·
> [Europe PMC PMC5665988](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC5665988)

The article is published under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
([terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). The licence statement is in the article's own
text, read from the Europe PMC full-text copy on 2026-11-13.

**Changes made:** the paper's methods and findings were condensed into claims, and invented characters
(Ines, Dot and Gyrodinium, the last named after the genus the paper identifies) and scenes were added to
carry them. A few lines are jokes. The authors did not write or endorse the storyboard or any comic made
from it. Any error in it is ours.

The storyboard and anything rendered from it are shared under CC BY 4.0, so the chain of attribution
stays unbroken. The paper itself is not copied into this repository.
